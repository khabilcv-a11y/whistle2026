How it would work
  • Placement A (recommended on desktop): a full band between the banner and the form. It builds trust right before people start typing, and adds about 520 px of scrolling.
  • Placement B: one rotating quote plus an Instagram button, about 90 px tall. Better if you want the form to stay as high as possible.
  • Managed by you: a new Stories tab in the admin portal, like Banners: photo link, quote, name, batch, and optional Instagram reel link.
  • Instagram: reels open on Instagram in a new tab (they cannot play inside the page without loading Instagram's heavy scripts), which also sends people to your other activities. A thumbnail image for each reel is optional.
  • Photos: square, at least 300 × 300 px, face in the centre. Without a photo the initials are shown.
