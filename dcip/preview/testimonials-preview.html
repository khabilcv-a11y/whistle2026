  • Why this does not crowd the form: the header and form stay exactly as they are. On screens wider than about 1280 px the card floats in the empty space beside the form and follows you as you scroll, so it is always visible but never in the way. On phones and tablets it is one small card below the Submit button, so people reach the form first.
  • The preview that invites a tap: a real picture from your latest reel or post, a small ▶ REEL tag, and "Open ↗". Up to three pictures fade one after another inside the same card. One tap opens your Instagram page or the post, in a new tab, so the form stays open.
  • Where the pictures come from: Instagram does not let other sites fetch its thumbnails automatically, and its own links to them expire. So you add, in a new admin tab, the post link, a picture link (a screenshot saved on ImgBB, Drive or GitHub, the same way as banners) and a short caption. About two minutes per post.
  • The lighter option: skip the card and keep only the small "📷 Follow on Instagram" chip in the header plus a footer link. Zero height, nothing to maintain. Full interns' stories would then stay on the DCIP website.
  • My suggestion: the floating card on desktop and the one small card on phones. Intern stories and long testimonials stay on www.dcip.co.in.
